Our Story

A Legacy of Flavours, Passed Through Generations

Founded in 2008 by the Mehra family, Saffron House began as a modest dining room in Bandra with a single iron kadhai and a grandmother's recipe book worn thin with use.

We source our spices directly from Rajasthani farms, grind our masalas fresh each morning, and slow-cook our signature curries in sealed handi pots for three full hours.

Chef Arjun MehraHead Chef & Co-Founder · 20 yrs experience
